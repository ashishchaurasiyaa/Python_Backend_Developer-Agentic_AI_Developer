
## 🍅 Pomodoro Discipline

```
Use a timer (physical or app like Focus Keeper):

50 min focus  →  10 min break  →  REPEAT

During focus:
   ✓ Phone in another room
   ✓ Notifications OFF
   ✓ Single tab/window
   ✓ No music with lyrics (instrumental OK)

During break:
   ✗ NO Instagram / YouTube / X
   ✓ Walk, water, stretch, deep breath
   ✓ Look at distant objects (eye health)

Long break (lunch) — 30-60 min:
   ✓ Real meal (not in front of laptop)
   ✓ Walk outside
   ✓ NO study material
```
