## 🧭 WHAT SHIFTED SINCE THE 5 JUL PULL

**New since July:**
1. **MCP's footprint held and grew**, not a fluke — this pull independently found 2 more explicit MCP mentions (Ebyte's full server/client architecture ask, Sapiens' MCP skill tag) on top of July's 9. Two independent pulls agreeing is a stronger signal than either alone.
2. **AWS Bedrock has caught up to Azure OpenAI** as the named managed-LLM platform of choice — July's sample skewed almost entirely Azure; this pull has real Bedrock depth (Ebyte's full AWS service list, hirezy.ai's Bedrock-only stack, 2 Capgemini reqs).
3. **"AI-first engineering" as a literal job requirement** — CLANX/Lumio's Senior Agentic AI Engineer JD requires "deep hands-on experience with Claude Code and Cursor in real-world software development" and tasks the hire with driving org-wide adoption of AI-assisted dev workflows. This is a different ask than "build AI products" — it's "use AI tools to build *any* software faster," now showing up as a formal senior-level requirement.
4. **New title patterns**: "Forward Deployed AI Engineer" (BCG X, consulting-embedded), "AgentOps" (Pearson), "AI Red Team" (Societe Generale) — all absent from the July sample, all suggesting the market is starting to specialize beyond the generic "GenAI Engineer" label.
5. **Comp spread widened at both ends.** High end: Aadmi's ₹45.6-57.7L remote GenAI role, Fulfil.IO's ₹55-80L pure-backend Senior Engineer role, Naukri's ₹30-65L Enterprise Architect GenAI listing. Low end: DTP Labs' ₹4.8-6L "Python & AI Backend Engineer," TestUnity-style sub-₹5L GenAI titles seen in earlier searches, TCS's ₹4-9L "Agentic AI Engineer" at 7-10 yrs. **Title inflation is visibly reaching junior/mid bands now** — a "GenAI Engineer" title no longer reliably signals senior-level comp.
6. **Non-metro cities show up in volume** once the search wasn't restricted to the original 4 locations: Bhopal (2 separate GenAI/backend hybrid roles), Rajkot, Jaipur, Kochi, Indore, Dehradun, Navi Mumbai. Partly an artifact of broadening the search itself, but genuinely shows this hiring wave isn't Bangalore/Pune/Hyderabad-exclusive anymore.
7. **Staffing/recruiting intermediaries are a bigger visible share of GenAI-titled postings** — ClanX itself is a recruiter, plus Naukri Assist, Saaki Argus & Averil, Rupa Career Solutions, hirezy.ai, and Weekday all showed up posting GenAI/Agentic roles on behalf of undisclosed or third-party clients. Worth remembering: raw posting counts overstate unique open roles when multiple staffing firms re-post the same underlying req.

**Confirmed / unchanged from July:**
1. **RAG + LangChain/LangGraph remain table stakes** for Track B — still true, still worth the dedicated prep time [`STUDY_PLAN.md`](STUDY_PLAN.md) already allocates.
2. **Django is not dead.** Still shows up across Track A in this broader, later pull (Siemens, Infosys ×3, Colan Infotech, Umruz, Aviso AI) — the January-side worry that FastAPI would fully displace it hasn't materialized.
3. **FastAPI remains the default choice for new backend work and virtually all Agentic/GenAI service layers** — unchanged.
4. **Senior/architect-level "Agentic AI Engineer" roles still diverge sharply from mid-level ones** — mid-level wants hands-on LangChain+RAG+prompt engineering; senior/architect wants agent evaluation, HITL design, AI governance. This pull adds "Forward Deployed" and "AI-first engineering leadership" as two more flavors of that same senior-level divergence, not a reversal of it.
5. **The line between "backend" and "AI engineer" postings keeps blurring**, and now more visibly outside the original 3 metro hubs too (Sia's Mumbai backend req explicitly lists AI/ML model integration as a core responsibility; DTP Labs' Delhi "backend" req requires LangChain).

## 📌 Notable individual patterns worth tracking

- **Companies running 2-4 concurrent reqs in the same city** are common across both tracks and both pulls: Clock Edge Technologies (3 GenAI reqs, Bangalore+Hyderabad), 100MS (2 AI-agents-healthcare reqs, Bangalore), Sia (3 reqs across its new Mumbai office — backend, GenAI, and from the earlier Track A search an Engineering Manager req too), Infosys (3 separate Python-backend reqs on Naukri alone, on top of 40 total). Multiple simultaneous reqs at one employer usually means active team-building, not backfill.
- **BCG X's "Forward Deployed AI Engineer"** posted for both Mumbai and Gurugram with identical JD text — worth watching as a role-type: global consultancies embedding engineers directly with clients for AI delivery, a step beyond traditional strategy consulting.
- **Staffing/recruiter-posted GenAI roles are numerous and worth reading skeptically** — several (Rupa Career Solutions, Saaki Argus & Averil, Naukri Assist, hirezy.ai) post wide experience bands and wide salary ranges ("5-8 yrs, ₹4.8-17.8L") that likely represent a template being reused across multiple actual client roles, not one specific opening.
- **Naukri's own aggregate numbers are the most useful "zoom out" data point from this whole pull**: 750 Python-backend postings vs 10,327 keyword-matched agentic/GenAI postings, roughly 14:1.
