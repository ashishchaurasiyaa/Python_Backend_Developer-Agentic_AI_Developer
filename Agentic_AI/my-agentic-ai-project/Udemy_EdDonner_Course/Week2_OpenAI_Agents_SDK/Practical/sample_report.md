## Mission Timeline
The Apollo 11 mission timeline began on July 16, 1969, and ended on July 24, 1969. The mission involved the first crewed lunar landing, which was achieved by Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin. The key events of the mission timeline include the launch of the spacecraft on July 16, 1969, the transit to the Moon, and the lunar landing on July 20, 1969. After spending some time on the Moon's surface, the astronauts returned to their spacecraft and lifted off from the Moon on July 21, 1969. They then transited back to Earth, splashing down in the Pacific Ocean on July 24, 1969.

The Apollo 11 Moon landing had a significant cultural impact, inspiring future generations and influencing popular culture.
