# Tesla — Financial Research Report
## Company Overview
Tesla, Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company that designs, manufactures, and sells battery electric vehicles, stationary battery energy storage devices, solar panels, and related products and services. The company is one of the world's most valuable companies in terms of market capitalization and has been the world's most valuable automaker since July 2020.

## History
The company was incorporated in July 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning as Tesla Motors, and its name is a tribute to the inventor and electrical engineer Nikola Tesla. Elon Musk led Tesla's first funding round in February 2004 and became the company's chairman, subsequently claiming to be a co-founder; in 2008, he was named chief executive officer. The company began production of its first car model, the Roadster sports car, in 2008, followed by the Model S sedan in 2012, the Model X SUV in 2015, the Model 3 sedan in 2017, the Model Y crossover in 2020, the Tesla Semi truck in 2022, and the Cybertruck pickup truck in 2023.

## Recent Events
From October 2021 to March 2022, Tesla was a US$1 trillion company, the sixth company to reach this milestone. The Tesla Model Y is a battery electric compact crossover SUV produced by Tesla, Inc. since 2020, and it is the best-selling electric vehicle of all time, having sold more than 2.16 million units worldwide. In 2023, Tesla delivered 1.2 million Model Ys, making it the world's best-selling vehicle that year, surpassing the Toyota Corolla and becoming the first electric vehicle to claim that title.

## Outlook
As the world's most valuable automaker and a leader in the electric vehicle market, Tesla is well-positioned for continued growth and success. The company's ability to innovate and adapt to changing market conditions will be crucial in maintaining its competitive edge. With a strong product lineup and a growing global presence, Tesla is likely to remain a major player in the automotive and clean energy industries for years to come.
