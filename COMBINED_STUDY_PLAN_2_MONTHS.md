**Reference:** [Backend_Developer/Phase8_Interview_Prep/12_negotiation_offer.md](Backend_Developer/Phase8_Interview_Prep/12_negotiation_offer.md)

### Key Tactics for AI Roles

```
1. AI premium = +20-30%
   AI-heavy role at Tier 1 should pay ₹40-55 LPA base
   Don't accept "senior backend" pay for AI work

2. NEVER give first number
   "I'm looking for market-competitive AI engineer comp"

3. STACK competing offers
   2-3 offers → bidding war
   Don't share exact numbers, share ranges

4. NEGOTIATE BEYOND BASE
   ✓ Joining bonus (₹5-15 L common)
   ✓ ESOPs / RSUs (4-year vest)
   ✓ Annual bonus (15-25% target)
   ✓ Sign-on milestones
   ✓ Relocation if applicable

5. THINK TOTAL COMP
   Total = Base + Bonus + Stock + Other
   ₹50 LPA base + ₹10 L stock + ₹5 L bonus = ₹65 LPA total

6. ALWAYS SLEEP ON IT
   "Can I get back in 48 hours?"
   Never accept on call
```
