## 🛠️ Kaise dekhein — sirf entertainment nahi, practice bhi

1. **Pehli baar:** English subtitles ON. Har movie se **5–10 naye phrases** nikaalo, `English_Daily_Practice.xlsx` ya [`word_of_the_day.md`](practice/word_of_the_day.md) me likho.
2. **2–3 din baad, dusri baar:** subtitles OFF — dekho kitna samajh aata hai bina padhe.
3. **Shadowing:** 2–3 chhoti scenes choose karo, pause karo, line ko **zor se bolo** — exact rhythm/stress match karne ki koshish karo. Yahi asli speaking practice hai, sirf sunna nahi.
4. **Tech-heavy movies** (Social Network, Steve Jobs, Moneyball, Imitation Game) se specifically dekho ki **technical/abstract cheezein conversationally kaise bolte hain** — seedha interview English (`03_Advanced/06_interview_english.md`) ke kaam aata hai.
