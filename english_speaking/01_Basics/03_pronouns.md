# 👥 Pronouns — I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Pronouns wo chhote words hain jo naam ki jagah aate hain. Inhe sahi use karna fluent bolne ke liye must hai — warna sentence turant ajeeb lagta hai.

## 🪪 Pronoun hota kya hai?

Naam baar-baar dohraane ki jagah hum pronoun use karte hain.

- ~~Ramesh opened Ramesh's laptop and Ramesh started Ramesh's work.~~ (bahut bura)
- **Ramesh opened his laptop and started his work.** (natural)

"Ramesh" ki jagah **he/his** aa gaye — yahi pronoun ka kaam hai.

## 1️⃣ Subject Pronouns (kaun kaam kar raha hai)

Ye sentence ke shuru mein, subject ki jagah aate hain.

| Subject pronoun | Hindi | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | main | **I** write code. |
| you | tum/aap | **You** are right. |
| he | woh (male) | **He** is my manager. |
| she | woh (female) | **She** leads the team. |
| it | yeh/woh (cheez) | **It** works perfectly. |
| we | hum | **We** ship on Fridays. |
| they | woh log | **They** joined last week. |

> ❌ Common mistake: "Me and my friend went there." (subject position mein "me" galat)
> ✅ Correct: "**My friend and I** went there." (subject ke liye "I", aur khud ko last mein politely rakho.)

## 2️⃣ Object Pronouns (kis pe kaam ho raha hai)

Ye verb ke baad ya preposition (to, for, with) ke baad aate hain.

| Subject | Object | Hindi | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **me** | mujhe | Call **me** later. |
| you | **you** | tumhe | I will help **you**. |
| he | **him** | usse (male) | Tell **him** the plan. |
| she | **her** | usse (female) | I asked **her**. |
| it | **it** | usko (cheez) | Restart **it**. |
| we | **us** | humein | Join **us** for lunch. |
| they | **them** | unhe | I emailed **them**. |

> ❌ Common mistake: "He gave the report to I." / "Come with we."
> ✅ Correct: "He gave the report to **me**." / "Come with **us**." — to/for/with ke baad object pronoun.

## 3️⃣ Possessive Adjectives vs Possessive Pronouns

Yahan log akser ulajh jaate hain. Dhyaan se.

### Possessive adjective — noun ke SAATH (kiska + cheez)
| Word | Hindi | Example |
|---|---|---|
| my | mera | This is **my** laptop. |
| your | tumhara | Where is **your** desk? |
| his | uska (male) | **His** code is clean. |
| her | uska (female) | **Her** idea is good. |
| its | uska (cheez) | The app lost **its** data. |
| our | hamara | **Our** team is small. |
| their | unka | **Their** project shipped. |

### Possessive pronoun — AKELE (noun ke bina, "...ka waala")
| Word | Hindi | Example |
|---|---|---|
| mine | mera | This laptop is **mine**. |
| yours | tumhara | The choice is **yours**. |
| his | uska | This seat is **his**. |
| hers | uska | That idea was **hers**. |
| ours | hamara | The win is **ours**. |
| theirs | unka | The fault is **theirs**. |

Farak samjho: **"my laptop"** (my ke baad noun zaroori) vs **"this laptop is mine"** (mine akela, noun nahi).

> ❌ Common mistake: "This is mine laptop." / "Is this your's?"
> ✅ Correct: "This is **my** laptop." / "Is this **yours**?" (yours mein apostrophe NAHI lagta.)

> ❌ Common mistake: "its" vs "it's" — "it's" matlab "it is", possessive nahi.
> ✅ Correct: "The app lost **its** data." (possessive, no apostrophe) vs "**It's** raining." (= it is).

## 4️⃣ Reflexive Pronouns (-self / -selves)

Jab subject aur object same hote hain (khud par kaam).

| Reflexive | Hindi | Example |
|---|---|---|
| myself | khud (main) | I taught **myself** Python. |
| yourself | khud (tum) | Be **yourself** in the interview. |
| himself | khud (woh male) | He fixed it **himself**. |
| herself | khud (woh female) | She did it **herself**. |
| itself | khud (cheez) | The script runs **itself**. |
| ourselves | khud (hum) | We solved it **ourselves**. |
| themselves | khud (woh log) | They organized it **themselves**. |

> ❌ Common mistake: "myself Rahul" (introduction mein — bahut common Indian galti)
> ✅ Correct: "**I am** Rahul." / "**My name is** Rahul." (introduction mein kabhi "myself" se shuru mat karo.)

## 5️⃣ Demonstratives: this / that / these / those

Ye kisi cheez ko point karte hain — paas ya door, ek ya zyada.

| Word | Distance | Number | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **this** | paas (near) | ek (singular) | **This** is my desk. |
| **that** | door (far) | ek (singular) | **That** is the server room. |
| **these** | paas (near) | zyada (plural) | **These** are my notes. |
| **those** | door (far) | zyada (plural) | **Those** are the old files. |

> ❌ Common mistake: "This are my files." / "These is good."
> ✅ Correct: "**These** are my files." / "**This** is good." — this/that → singular, these/those → plural.

## 6️⃣ Kab "it" use karein

"It" un cheezon, jaanwaron (general), weather, time, aur situations ke liye aata hai — insaan ke liye nahi.

- The build failed. **It** took an hour to fix.
- **It** is raining. / **It** is 9 o'clock. / **It** is Monday.
- The function returns a value. **It** is cached.

> ❌ Common mistake: insaan ke liye "it" — "My manager is nice, it helps me."
> ✅ Correct: "My manager is nice, **she/he** helps me." (insaan ke liye he/she.)

## ⚠️ Common confusion: he / she mix-up

Hindi mein "woh" male/female dono ke liye same hai, isliye Indians bolte waqt **he/she galat aapas mein badal dete hain** — chahe wo grammar samajhte ho. Ye sirf aadat ki galti hai, dhyaan se theek hoti hai.

- Female ke liye → **she, her, hers, herself**.
- Male ke liye → **he, him, his, himself**.

> ❌ Common mistake: "My sister is a doctor. He works in Delhi." / "My brother... she said..."
> ✅ Correct: "My sister is a doctor. **She** works in Delhi." / "My brother... **he** said..."

Practice tip: jab bhi kisi female ke baare mein bolo, dimaag mein "she-her" tag laga lo; male ke liye "he-him". Bolte waqt ek pal ruk kar sahi pronoun chuno — speed baad mein aayegi.

## ✅ Quick recap

- Subject: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.
- Object: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
- Possessive adj (noun ke saath): my, your, his, her, its, our, their.
- Possessive pronoun (akela): mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.
- Reflexive: myself, yourself, himself... Introduction mein "I am", "myself" nahi.
- this/that = singular, these/those = plural.
- Insaan ke liye he/she — female=she, male=he. Mix mat karo.

## 🎤 Practice (zor se bolo)

Zor se, saaf bolo. Pronoun pe dhyaan do.

1. **She** is my team lead, and **I** report to **her**.
2. **He** sent **me** the document; I thanked **him**.
3. This laptop is **mine**, and that one is **yours**.
4. **Our** team finished early, but **their** team is still working.
5. I taught **myself** system design, and she prepared **herself** for the interview.
6. **These** are my notes, and **those** are the old slides.
7. **It** is raining, and **it** is already 8 o'clock.
8. **They** invited **us** to the demo, so **we** joined **them**.
9. My sister is an engineer; **she** works at a startup.
10. My brother loves cricket; **he** plays every Sunday.

Fix karo:
11. "Me and him went to the meeting." → ***He and I* went to the meeting.*
12. "My mother is teacher, he teaches maths." → *My mother is **a teacher**; **she** teaches maths.*

🗣️ **Mini speaking task:** Apne ghar ya team ke 4 logon ka 2-2 sentence introduction bolo, sahi he/she/his/her use karte hue. Jaise — "This is my sister. She is a designer. Her work is creative." Female ke liye she-her, male ke liye he-him pakka rakho.
