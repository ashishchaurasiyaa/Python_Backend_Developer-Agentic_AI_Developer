# 🎙️ Basic Speaking Drills — roz bolne ki aadat

English bolna ek skill hai, theory nahi. Jaise gym mein muscle banti hai, waise hi roz bolne se zubaan khulti hai. Ye lesson 100% practice ke liye hai — har cheez ZOR SE bolne ke liye banai gayi hai.

> Sabse bada raaz: **roz 10 minute bolna**, hafte mein ek baar 2 ghante se behtar hai. Consistency > perfection. Galti karo, par bolo.

## 🌞 Daily self-talk routine (subah ki narration)

Subah uthkar, apne aap se English mein baat karo. Koi sun nahi raha — isliye darr nahi. Ye lines roz bolo:

- I wake up at six in the morning.
- I get out of bed and stretch a little.
- I brush my teeth and wash my face.
- I make a cup of tea.
- I check my phone and read my messages.
- I take a shower and get dressed.
- I have my breakfast.
- I open my laptop and start my work.

Pattern dekho: sab **"I + verb + ..."** (present simple). Yahi structure baar-baar bolne se automatic ho jaata hai.

> ❌ Common mistake: "I am wake up at six." / "I doing brush."
> ✅ Correct: "I **wake up** at six." / "I **brush** my teeth." (routine = present simple, plain verb.)

## 🗣️ "Narrate what you're doing" technique

Ye sabse powerful aadat hai. Jo bhi kaam kar rahe ho, usko English mein bol-bol kar batao — jaise commentary. Akele ho to loud, public mein ho to mann mein.

Abhi ke moment ke liye **present continuous** (am/is/are + verb-ing) use hota hai:

- I am reading an English lesson right now.
- I am sitting at my desk.
- I am drinking water.
- I am opening my laptop.
- I am writing some code.
- I am thinking about the solution.

> ❌ Common mistake: "I reading the lesson." / "I am think about it."
> ✅ Correct: "I **am reading** the lesson." (am/is/are + verb**-ing** zaroori) / "I **am thinking** about it."

Roz ke kaam ko narrate karo: chai banate waqt — "I am boiling the water. I am adding tea leaves. I am pouring it into a cup." Din mein 20 baar aisa karoge to bolna natural ho jaayega.

## 📋 30 ready-to-speak sentences (daily life)

Inhe roz bolo. Block ke block padho, ZOR SE.

**Morning (subah):**
1. I get up early and feel fresh.
2. I drink a glass of water first.
3. I go for a short walk.
4. I have a light breakfast.
5. I plan my tasks for the day.

**Work (kaam):**
6. I join the morning standup.
7. I check my emails and messages.
8. I work on my main task first.
9. I take a short break every hour.
10. I ask my teammate for help when I am stuck.
11. I write clean and simple code.
12. I test my changes before I push them.
13. I attend a meeting in the afternoon.
14. I update my manager on the progress.
15. I finish my work and log off in the evening.

**Daily life (roz ki zindagi):**
16. I cook dinner with my family.
17. I call my parents in the evening.
18. I watch a show to relax.
19. I read a few pages before bed.
20. I go to sleep around eleven.

**Feelings & opinions (bhaav aur raay):**
21. I feel confident about my work today.
22. I think this approach is better.
23. I am happy with the result.
24. I am a little tired, but I am okay.
25. I want to improve my English.

**Plans (future):**
26. I will finish this task tomorrow.
27. I am going to learn system design this month.
28. I plan to take a short break this weekend.
29. I will call you after the meeting.
30. I am going to give my best in the interview.

## 🪑 Describe your room / desk

Apne aas-paas ki cheezein describe karna confidence build karta hai. Ye template bolo, apni cheezon ke saath:

- This is my room. It is small but tidy.
- There is a desk near the window.
- On the desk, there is a laptop, a notebook, and a cup of tea.
- My chair is comfortable.
- There are some books on the shelf.
- The light comes from the window on the left.
- I like my workspace because it is quiet.

> ❌ Common mistake: "There is many books." / "It have a window."
> ✅ Correct: "There **are** many books." (plural → are) / "It **has** a window."

"There is / There are" yaad rakho: **ek cheez → there is**, **zyada cheezein → there are**.

## 🙋 Simple self-introduction template (fill in the blanks)

Interview aur naye log se milne mein ye sabse zaroori hai. Apne details bhar ke, roz bolo jab tak rat na jaaye:

> Hello, my name is **________**.
> I am from **________** (city).
> I am a **________** (job title, e.g. backend developer).
> I have **________** years of experience.
> I work with **________** (technologies, e.g. Python, Java, PostgreSQL).
> Currently, I am working on **________** (project / area).
> In my free time, I like **________** (hobby).
> I am preparing for senior backend roles, and I want to grow as an engineer.
> Thank you.

Example bhara hua:

> Hello, my name is Rahul. I am from Pune. I am a backend developer. I have five years of experience. I work with Python, Django, and PostgreSQL. Currently, I am working on a payments service. In my free time, I like reading and cricket. I am preparing for senior backend roles, and I want to grow as an engineer. Thank you.

> ❌ Common mistake: "Myself Rahul." / "I am having 5 years experience." / "I am working since 5 years."
> ✅ Correct: "**My name is** Rahul." / "I **have** 5 years **of** experience." / "I **have been working for** 5 years."

## 📆 7-Day Mini Speaking Challenge

Har din ek chhota target. Roz kam-se-kam **10 minute** loud bolo. Ho sake to phone par record karo aur wapas suno — apni galti khud sunai degi.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Subah ka self-talk routine (upar wali 8 lines) 3 baar bolo. |
| **Day 2** | Apna din narrate karo: subah se raat tak 10 sentences "I + verb" mein. |
| **Day 3** | "Narrate what you're doing" — koi 1 kaam (chai banana / kapde rakhna) continuous mein bolo. |
| **Day 4** | Apna desk/room describe karo (template use karo), 6+ sentences. |
| **Day 5** | Self-introduction 5 baar bolo, har baar थोड़ा smooth karte hue. Record karo. |
| **Day 6** | 30 ready sentences mein se koi 15 chuno aur bina dekhe bolo. |
| **Day 7** | Sab mila ke: 2-minute self-talk — apna intro + aaj ka din + kal ka plan, ek saans-flow mein. |

Saat din baad ye challenge dobara karo — har round pe aap pehle se behtar honge.

## ✅ Quick recap

- Roz 10 minute loud bolna sabse important aadat hai.
- Routine = present simple ("I wake up"), abhi-ka-kaam = present continuous ("I am reading").
- "Narrate what you're doing" se zubaan automatic khulti hai.
- "There is / there are" aur self-intro template rat lo.
- 7-day challenge ko cycle ki tarah dohrao.

## 🎤 Practice (zor se bolo)

Ye sab abhi, isi waqt, ZOR SE bolo:

1. I wake up at six and drink a glass of water.
2. I am sitting at my desk and reading this lesson right now.
3. I check my emails first and then start my main task.
4. There is a laptop and a notebook on my desk.
5. There are some books on the shelf behind me.
6. My name is ____, and I am a backend developer. (apna naam bharo)
7. I have ____ years of experience. (number bharo)
8. I am working on ____ these days. (project bharo)
9. I will finish my task tomorrow and call you after the meeting.
10. I want to improve my English, so I practise every day.
11. I am thinking about the solution, and I feel confident.
12. In my free time, I like ____. (hobby bharo)

🗣️ **Mini speaking task:** Phone ka voice recorder kholo aur 60 second tak bina ruke apne aaj ke din ke baare mein English mein bolo — subah se ab tak kya kiya, ab kya kar rahe ho, aage kya plan hai. Phir recording suno, ek galti dhoondho, aur dobara bolkar theek karo. Ye roz karo — yahi asli "padha → kiya" practice hai.
