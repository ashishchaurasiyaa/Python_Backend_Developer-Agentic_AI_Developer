## ✅ Quick recap

- Vocabulary theme ke saath yaad rakho — number, time, family, verbs, food, body, home, clothes, colours, adjectives, places, office, question words.

## 🎤 Practice (zor se bolo)

Niche ke har sentence zor se bolo, aur blank bhar ke apna version banao.

1. I have ____ meetings today. (number bharo)
2. We have a standup every ____. (din bharo)
3. My ____ is a doctor. (family member)
4. I need to ____ the bug before lunch. (verb)
5. I usually eat ____ for breakfast. (food)
6. My favourite colour is ____. (colour)
7. This task is ____ but important. (adjective)
8. Turn ____ at the signal to reach the office. (direction)
9. The ____ is tomorrow, so I am busy. (office word)
10. ____ is the status of the deployment? (question word)
11. My ____ is supportive and gives good feedback. (office word)
12. I ____ to the gym in the evening. (verb)

🗣️ **Mini speaking task:** Ek hafte ke liye roz 1 table chuno (jaise "common verbs") aur uske saare example sentences ZOR SE bolo. Phir bina table dekhe, us theme ke 5 naye sentences khud banao. Saat din mein 5+ themes cover ho jayenge.
