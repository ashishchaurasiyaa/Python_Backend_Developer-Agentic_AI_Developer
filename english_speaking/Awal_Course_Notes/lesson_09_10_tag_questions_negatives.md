# 🎓 Lesson 9 & 10 (Awal Sir) — Tag Questions & Negative Sentences

> Source: `LESSON 9 & 10. - Tag questions & negative sentences.pptx`. Teen parts hain — (1) logon ke baare mein sawaal poochne ke 4 tarike (yes/no, information, WH-word, tag questions), (2) 'the' (definite article) ka use, (3) negative sentences banana.
> 'the' ka deep-dive already available hai [01_Basics/02_grammar_basics.md#🟣-articles-a--an--the](../01_Basics/02_grammar_basics.md) mein — yahan sirf slide ka recap hai.

## 1️⃣ Simple Yes/No Questions

Meaning: Jin sawaalon ka jawaab sirf "yes" ya "no" mein diya ja sake.

| Example | Practice |
|---|---|
| Is she your friend? | Is he a teacher? |
| Do you like pizza? | Do you have a pet? |
| Are they from France? | Are they brothers? |

🗣️ **Try it**: Is/Do/Are se 3 apne sawaal banao, khud ko jawaab do.

## 2️⃣ Information Questions

Meaning: Jo sawaal specific information maangte hain (haan/na se jawaab nahi ban sakta).

| Example | Practice |
|---|---|
| What is her name? | What is your favorite color? |
| Where do you live? | Where did you go on vacation? |
| How old are they? | How many siblings do you have? |

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne kisi dost ke baare mein 3 information questions poochho.

## 3️⃣ Questions with WH-Words

Meaning: Who, what, where, when, why, how se shuru hone wale sawaal — pehle bhi [lesson_07_08](lesson_07_08_pronouns_wh_questions.md) mein detail se cover ho chuke hain, yahan revision hai.

| Example | Practice |
|---|---|
| Who is your best friend? | Who is your favorite actor? |
| What do you like to do? | What time do you wake up in the morning? |
| Where did you meet them? | Why do you like that book? |

## 4️⃣ Tag Questions — naya topic

Meaning: Statement ke end mein chhota sawaal jodte hain — confirm karne ya agreement maangne ke liye. Hindi mein iske barabar "na?" / "hai na?" jaisa feel hai.

**Rule (simple version):** Statement **positive** ho → tag **negative** hota hai. Statement **negative** ho → tag **positive** hota hai.

| Statement | Tag | Full sentence |
|---|---|---|
| You like ice cream (positive) | don't you? | You like ice cream, don't you? |
| She is your sister (positive) | isn't she? | She is your sister, isn't she? |
| He can swim (positive) | can't he? | He can swim, can't he? |

🗣️ **Try it (practice questions se)**:
1. You enjoy watching movies, don't you?
2. She speaks Spanish, doesn't she?
3. He plays the guitar, doesn't he?

> ⚠️ Pattern yaad rakho: statement ka verb pakdo (is/can/like...), uska negative short-form tag mein lagao + pronoun. "You like" → "don't you?" (like ke liye do/does/did use hota hai).

## 5️⃣ Use of 'The' (definite article) — quick recap

'The' ek specific, particular cheez/vyakti point out karta hai — jaise spotlight kisi ek cheez par. Poora deep-dive [01_Basics/02_grammar_basics.md](../01_Basics/02_grammar_basics.md) mein hai; slide ke examples:

- The cat is sleeping on the couch.
- Could you pass me **the** salt, please?
- The teacher is very kind.
- The sun is shining brightly today.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne ghar ke 3 cheezon ko "the" ke saath describe karo (jaise "The TV is in the living room").

## 6️⃣ Negative Sentences — naya topic

**Structure:** Subject + do/does/did + **not** + base form of verb

| Positive | Negative |
|---|---|
| She likes coffee. | She does **not** (doesn't) like coffee. |
| He eats meat. | He does **not** (doesn't) eat meat. |
| They play basketball. | They do **not** (don't) play basketball. |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Contradiction batana | He doesn't eat meat. / They don't play basketball. |
| Negative response dena | No, I don't have a cat. / No, she doesn't know him. |
| Negative statement banana (is/was ke saath) | The weather isn't nice today. / The movie wasn't interesting. |

### Common negative words

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| **Not** | I do not (don't) understand. |
| **No** | No, she doesn't like chocolate. |
| **Never** | They never go to the beach. |
| **None** | None of them want to come. |
| **Neither...nor** | Neither of them speaks French. |

🗣️ **Try it**: never, none, neither...nor — har ek se 1-1 apna sentence banao.

## ✍️ Practice Exercises (slide se)

**A. In negative form rewrite karo:**
1. She likes ice cream. → ______
2. He goes to school every day. → ______
3. They visited the museum last week. → ______
4. I have a sister. → ______
5. We play soccer in the park. → ______

**Answers:** 1. She does not (doesn't) like ice cream. 2. He does not (doesn't) go to school every day. 3. They did not (didn't) visit the museum last week. 4. I do not (don't) have a sister. 5. We do not (don't) play soccer in the park.

**B. Fill in the blanks:**
1. I have ____ seen that movie. (never)
2. She ____ like vegetables. (doesn't)
3. ____ of them can swim. (None)
4. They are ____ coming to the party. (not)
5. He speaks ____ French ____ Spanish. (neither / nor)

## 📝 Homework (slide se)

Apni daily routine ke baare mein 5 negative sentences likho. (Jaise: "I don't wake up early on Sundays.")

## ✅ Quick recap

- Yes/No questions → is/do/are se shuru, jawaab yes/no.
- Information questions → What/Where/How se specific info maango.
- Tag questions → positive statement + negative tag (ya ulta). "You like ice cream, don't you?"
- The → ek specific/particular cheez point out karta hai.
- Negative sentences → do/does/did + not + base verb. Words: not, no, never, none, neither...nor.

## 🎤 Speaking practice (zor se bolo)

1. Is he a teacher, or is he an engineer?
2. What time do you wake up, and where do you work?
3. You like coffee, don't you? — Yes, but I don't drink it at night.
4. She doesn't eat meat, and she never eats fast food either.
5. None of my friends can swim, and neither can I.
6. The weather isn't nice today, but the meeting is still happening.
