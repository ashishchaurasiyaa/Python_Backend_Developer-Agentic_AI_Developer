## 1️⃣ Personal Pronouns (He / She / It / They)

Noun ki jagah use hote hain — sentence repetitive nahi lagta.

| Pronoun | Kab | Example |
|---|---|---|
| He | male person/animal | He is my brother. |
| She | female person/animal | She is my sister. |
| It | animal, object, concept | It is a cat. |
| They | ek se zyada | They are my friends. |

🗣️ **Try it**: He, She, It, They — har ek se 2-2 apne sentences banao.

## 2️⃣ Demonstrative Pronouns (This / That / These / Those)

Specific cheez ya group ko point out karte hain.

| Pronoun | Distance | Number | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| This | paas (near) | singular | This is my book. |
| That | door (far) | singular | That is my car. |
| These | paas (near) | plural | These are my shoes. |
| Those | door (far) | plural | Those are my friends. |

🗣️ **Try it**: This, That, These, Those — har ek se 2-2 apne sentences banao.

## 3️⃣ WH Questions — 6 types

Questions poochna information gather karne ke liye zaroori hai.

| Question word | Kis baare mein | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | cheez/info | What is your name? / What is this? / What are you doing? |
| **Where** | location | Where do you live? / Where is the library? / Where is my book? |
| **When** | time | When is your birthday? / When does the train arrive? |
| **Who** | person/identity | Who is your teacher? / Who is that person? / Who are your friends? |
| **How** | method/manner/condition | How are you? / How do you travel to work? / How can I help you? |
| **Why** | reason | Why do you like ice cream? / Why are you late? / Why did you choose this book? |

## 🎯 Activity (slide 2 se)

Apne room ko describe karo kisi friend ko — personal pronouns aur demonstrative pronouns dono use karo. Jaise: "This is my bed. It's very comfortable."

## ✅ Quick recap

- Personal: He/She (person), It (thing), They (plural).
- Demonstrative: This/That = singular, These/Those = plural. This/These = paas, That/Those = door.
- WH: What (cheez), Where (jagah), When (samay), Who (vyakti), How (tareeka), Why (reason).

## 🎤 Speaking practice (zor se bolo)

1. This is my room. It's small but comfortable.
2. Those are my books, and these are my notes.
3. What is your name, and where do you live?
4. Who is your best friend, and why do you like them?
5. How do you travel to work, and when do you leave home?
6. She is my sister; she is a doctor, and he is my brother; he is an engineer.
