# 🎓 Lesson 5 & 6 (Awal Sir) — Describing State/Condition + is/are

> Source: `LESSON 5 & 6 (1).pptx`. Do parts hain — (1) kisi ko describe karna commas/not/and se, (2) 'is'/'are' aur unke contractions.

## 1️⃣ Commas — list banane ke liye

Comma se ek sentence mein multiple cheezein/qualities list karte hain.

- She is tall, friendly, and intelligent.
- He has a cat, a dog, and a bird.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne aap ko 3 adjectives se describe karo, commas ke saath.

## 2️⃣ "Not" — negation ke liye

"Not" kisi statement ko deny/negate karta hai.

- She is **not** happy. (= she is unhappy)
- He is **not** going to the party. (= he is staying home)

🗣️ **Try it**: "not" use karke 2 sentences banao.

## 3️⃣ "And" — do cheezein jodne ke liye

- She is tall **and** intelligent.
- He likes to read **and** write.

🗣️ **Try it**: "and" use karke apne baare mein 2 sentences banao.

## 4️⃣ State/Condition describe karna

Common adjectives: happy, sad, tired, energetic, busy, relaxed, stressed, calm, excited, worried, confident, nervous.

- She is happy and excited about her new job.
- He is tired and needs some rest.

🗣️ **Try it**: Abhi apni state/condition ko 2 adjectives se describe karo.

## ✍️ Practice Exercise (slide se)

Fill in the blanks:
1. She is _____, _____, and _____.
2. He is _____ and _____, but not _____.

**Answers (class ke):**
1. She is tall, friendly, and intelligent.
2. He is busy and stressed, but not unhappy.

## 5️⃣ 'is' — Singular form

'to be' verb ka singular form — ek person/thing/situation ke liye.

| Example | Contraction |
|---|---|
| She is my sister. | She's my sister. |
| It is raining outside. | It's raining outside. |
| He is a good friend. | He's a good friend. |
| This book is interesting. | — |

🗣️ **Try it**: 'is' use karke 2 apne sentences banao.

## 6️⃣ 'are' — Plural form

'to be' verb ka plural form — ek se zyada logon/cheezon ke liye.

| Example | Contraction |
|---|---|
| They are my friends. | They're my friends. |
| We are studying English. | We're learning English. |
| The dogs are playing in the park. | — |

🗣️ **Try it**: 'are' use karke 2 apne sentences banao.

## 7️⃣ Basic Contractions

| Full form | Contraction | Example |
|---|---|---|
| is + not | isn't | She isn't here. / It isn't raining. |
| are + not | aren't | They aren't coming to the party. / We aren't going to the beach. |
| am + not | I'm not *(sirf "I" ke saath)* | I'm not feeling well. / I'm not interested in that movie. |
| will + not | won't | She won't be late. / They won't go to the concert. |

> ⚠️ Note: "amn't" jaisa koi word nahi hota — "am not" ka sirf ek hi short form hai: **I'm not**.

🗣️ **Try it**: isn't, aren't, I'm not, won't — har ek se 1-1 apna sentence banao (4 sentences).

## ✅ Quick recap

- Comma → list. Not → negate. And → combine.
- is → singular (he/she/it). are → plural (they/we/you).
- Contractions: isn't, aren't, I'm not, won't.

## 🎤 Speaking practice (zor se bolo)

1. She is tall, friendly, and intelligent.
2. He is busy and stressed, but not unhappy.
3. I'm not feeling well today, but I'm not worried.
4. They aren't coming to the party; they're studying.
5. It's raining outside, and she isn't happy about it.
6. We're learning English, and we won't give up.
