> Source: `LESSON 13 & 14 H. Preposition & quantifiers.pptx`. Do parts hain — (1) prepositions of time/place: in, on, at, (2) quantifiers: some, a lot of, many, much, few, little, enough, aren't/isn't, is there?/are there?. Sabse bada lesson ab tak — 5 exercises hain.
> Prepositions ka deep-dive [02_Intermediate/02_grammar_intermediate.md](../02_Intermediate/02_grammar_intermediate.md) mein bhi hai.

## 1️⃣ In / On / At — quick rule table (sabhi examples se nikala gaya)

| | Time | Place |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | months, years, seasons, parts of day (in the morning) | ander/enclosed space (in the box, in the room, in a city) |
| **on** | days, dates (on Monday, on 25th) | surface (on the table, on the wall, on the shelf) |
| **at** | precise clock time, "at night" | specific point (at the bus stop, at the corner, at the airport) |

### In — ander/within

- The cat is **in** the box.
- She lives **in** a small apartment.
- We have a meeting **in** the conference room.
- I'm interested **in** learning new languages.
- She was lost **in** thought.

### On — surface / specific day

- The cup is **on** the table.
- The poster is **on** the wall.
- The meeting is **on** Monday.
- The event is **on** the 10th of June.

### At — specific point/place

- She's waiting **at** the bus stop.
- He's good **at** playing the guitar.
- We met **at** the coffee shop.
- He's an expert **at** solving puzzles.

🗣️ **Homework (slide se)**: 5-5 sentences banao 'in', 'on', aur 'at' se — zor se practice karo.

## 2️⃣ Quantifiers — kitna/kitne batana

| # | Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Some** | thoda amount/number (positive sentences) | I have some apples in my bag. |
| 2 | **A lot of** | bahut zyada quantity | There are a lot of birds in the sky. |
| 3 | **Many** | bahut zyada, **countable** nouns ke saath | How many students are in your class? |
| 4 | **Much** | bahut zyada, **uncountable** nouns ke saath | I don't have much time today. |
| 5 | **Few** | thode, **countable** (kam) | Few people attended the meeting. |
| 6 | **Little** | thoda, **uncountable** (kam) | I have little money left. |
| 7 | **Enough** | kaafi/sufficient | Do you have enough time to finish the task? |
| 8 | **Aren't/Isn't** | are not / is not (negative) | They aren't coming to the party. She isn't feeling well. |
| 9 | **Is there?** | ek cheez exist karti hai kya (singular/uncountable) | Is there a park here? |
| 10 | **Are there?** | multiple cheezein exist karti hain kya (plural) | Are there any apples in the fridge? |

## 🔑 Countable vs Uncountable — yahi asli confusion hai

| | Countable (many, few, a number of) | Uncountable (much, little, a lot of) |
|---|---|---|
| Example nouns | books, students, cars, apples | water, time, money, information, sugar |
| Quantifier | many books, few students | much time, little money |
| Both use | **a lot of** books / **a lot of** time (a lot of dono ke saath chalta hai) | |

> ❌ Common mistake: "much apples" (apples countable hai, "much" nahi chalega)
> ✅ Correct: "**many** apples" / "**much** money"

## ✍️ Quantifiers Exercise 1: Fill in the Blanks

1. There isn't ___ milk left in the fridge. → **any** *(negative + uncountable)*
2. I have ___ friends in this city. → **some**
3. Do you have ___ questions about the lesson? → **any** *(question form)*
4. She has read ___ books about history. → **many / several**
5. We don't have ___ time to complete the project. → **much**
6. There are too ___ people in the room. → **many**
7. He spends ___ of his time playing video games. → **most**
8. ___ student in the class received a textbook. → **Every**
9. I only have ___ money, so I can't buy the expensive dress. → **little**
10. ___ of my parents support my decision. → **Both**

## ✍️ Quantifiers Exercise 2: Choose the Correct Option

1. There are ___ apples in the basket. → **many** *(countable)*
2. He has ___ patience with children. → **little** *(uncountable)*
3. I don't have ___ sugar left. → **any** *(negative)*
4. We have ___ time before the meeting starts. → **a lot of** *(time = uncountable, "many" galat hoga)*
5. ___ of them completed the assignment. → **All** *(ya "Each" — dono grammatically sahi hain, thoda alag matlab: "All" = group ek saath, "Each" = ek-ek individually)*

## ✍️ Quantifiers Exercise 3: Correct the Mistakes

| Galat | Sahi | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| She didn't have some money with her. | She didn't have **any** money with her. | Negative sentence mein "some" nahi, "any" chahiye |
| There is many information in the report. | There is **much** information in the report. *(ya "a lot of")* | "information" uncountable hai — "many" nahi chalta |
| I saw little people at the concert. | I saw **few** people at the concert. | "people" countable hai — "little" nahi, "few" chahiye |
| Every students must submit their homework. | Every **student** must submit their homework. | "Every" ke baad hamesha **singular** noun aata hai |
| I don't have much apples at home. | I don't have **many** apples at home. | "apples" countable hai — "much" nahi, "many" chahiye |

## 🎤 Speaking Activity: "Would You Rather?" (Pair Discussion)

Quantifiers use karke apni choice justify karo. Format: **"I would rather [option A] because [reason]. If [option B], then [downside]."**

**Questions:**
1. Have a lot of money or a few close friends?
2. Have some free time every day or none on weekdays but a lot of time on weekends?
3. Eat many small meals a day or a little food at each meal?
4. Travel to many countries or live in one amazing place forever?
5. Have several pets or none at all?

**Example:**
> Q: Would you rather eat many small meals a day or a little food at each meal?
> A: "I would rather eat many small meals a day because I feel more energetic. If I eat just a little food, I might feel hungry quickly."

🗣️ **Try it**: In 5 mein se 2 questions choose karo, apna answer quantifier ke saath do (many/a lot of/little/none/several).

## ✅ Quick recap

- **in** → months/years/enclosed space. **on** → days/dates/surface. **at** → precise time/specific point. Exception: **at night**.
- Public transport (bus/train/plane) → **on**, car → **in**.
- **Countable** nouns → many, few, a number of. **Uncountable** nouns → much, little. **A lot of** dono ke saath chalta hai.
- Negative/question sentences → **any** (not "some").
- **Every + singular noun** (not plural).
