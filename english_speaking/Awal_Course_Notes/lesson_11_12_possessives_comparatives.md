# 🎓 Lesson 11 & 12 (Awal Sir) — Possessive Pronouns/Adjectives + Comparative Adjectives

> Source: `LESSON 11 & 12. Possessive pronouns & adjectives.pptx`. Do parts hain — (1) possessive pronouns (my/mine, who's/whose, hers/his, yours/theirs, ours), (2) comparative adjectives (rules + irregular forms).
> Deep-dive possessive adjective vs pronoun table already hai [01_Basics/03_pronouns.md](../01_Basics/03_pronouns.md) (section 3) — ye file isi class ke slides ka quick recap + practice hai.

## 1️⃣ My / Mine

**My** = possessive **adjective** (noun ke saath aata hai). **Mine** = possessive **pronoun** (akela, noun ke bina, emphasis ke liye).

- This is **my** book. (my + noun)
- The blue backpack is **mine**. (mine akela — noun nahi)

## 2️⃣ Who's / Whose

⚠️ Ye sabse zyada confuse hone wala pair hai — dono sunne mein same lagte hain.

| Word | Kya hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Who's** | Contraction — "who is" ya "who has" | Who's coming to the party? (= Who is coming?) |
| **Whose** | Ownership poochne ke liye | Whose notebook is this? (kiski notebook hai) |

> ❌ Common mistake: "Who's bag is this?" (galat — ownership poochte waqt "whose" chahiye, "who's" nahi)
> ✅ Correct: "**Whose** bag is this?"

## 3️⃣ Hers / His

- **Hers** — female ke liye. "The pink dress is hers."
- **His** — male ke liye. "His hat is on the table."

## 4️⃣ Yours / Theirs

- **Yours** — ek person ke liye. "Is this pen yours?"
- **Theirs** — ek se zyada logon ke liye. "The red car is theirs."

## 5️⃣ Ours

Group/collective ownership ke liye. "The classroom is ours." / "The project is ours to complete."

## ✍️ Practice (slide se)

1. I left (my/mine) phone on the table. → **my**
2. (Who's/Whose) bag is this? → **Whose**
3. The cat is (hers/whose). → **hers**
4. Is this book (yours/my)? → **yours**
5. This garden is (ours). → **ours**

🗣️ **Try it**: In 5 sentences ko zor se bolo, phir 3 apne khud ke sentences banao — my/mine, whose, aur ours use karke.

## 6️⃣ Comparative Adjectives — do cheezon ko compare karna

### Rule 1 — One-syllable adjectives → add **-er**

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| soft | softer |
| cheap | cheaper |
| sweet | sweeter |
| thin | thinner |

### Spelling rules (one-syllable)

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Single vowel + single consonant → consonant **double** hota hai | thin → thin**n**er, big → big**g**er |
| Adjective **-e** se khatam ho → -e **hata** do, phir -er | wide → wid**er** |
| Adjective consonant+**-y** se khatam ho → **-y → -i**, phir -er | dry → dr**i**er |

### Rule 2 — Two-syllable adjectives ending in **-y** → -y→-i, add **-er**

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| lucky | luckier |
| pretty | prettier |
| tidy | tidier |

### Rule 3 — Two-syllable adjectives ending in **-ed, -ing, -ful, -less** → use **more**

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| worried | more worried |
| boring | more boring |
| careful | more careful |
| useless | more useless |

### Rule 4 — Three+ syllable adjectives → always **more**

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| dangerous | more dangerous |
| difficult | more difficult |

### Rule 5 — Irregular adjectives (yaad karo, koi rule nahi)

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| good | better |
| bad | worse |
| far | farther / further |

### Usage — comparatives ke saath **"than"** aata hai

- John is taller **than** me.
- I think that she's more intelligent **than** her sister.

## 💡 Bonus: Superlative (sabse zyada) — exercise mein chahiye hoga

Comparative = **do** cheezon mein compare. Superlative = **teen ya zyada** mein sabse best/worst — hamesha **"the"** ke saath aata hai.

| Type | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| One syllable | -er | **the** ___-**est** (big → the biggest) |
| Two syllable -y | -ier | **the** ___-**iest** (lucky → the luckiest) |
| Two/three+ syllable (more) | more ___ | **the most** ___ |
| Irregular | better/worse | **the best** / **the worst** |

## ✍️ Practice Exercise (slide se — comparative ya superlative dono chahiye)

1. My house is (big) than yours. → **bigger**
2. This flower is (beautiful) than that one. → **more beautiful**
3. This is the (interesting) book I have ever read. → **most interesting**
4. Non-smokers usually live (long) than smokers. → **longer**
5. Which is the (dangerous) animal in the world? → **most dangerous**
6. A holiday by the sea is (good) than a holiday in the mountains. → **better**
7. It is strange but often a coke is (expensive) than a beer. → **more expensive**
8. Who is the (rich) woman on earth? → **richest**
9. The weather this summer is even (bad) than last summer. → **worse**
10. He was the (clever) thief of all. → **cleverest**

## ✅ Quick recap

- my/whose/his/your/our + noun → possessive **adjective**. mine/whose/hers/yours/theirs/ours akele → possessive **pronoun**.
- who's = who is/has (contraction). whose = ownership poochna.
- Comparative: 1-syllable → -er. -y ending 2-syllable → -ier. -ed/-ing/-ful/-less aur 3+ syllable → more. Irregular yaad karo (good→better, bad→worse, far→farther/further).
- Comparative ke baad "than" aata hai. Superlative hamesha "the" + -est/most.

## 🎤 Speaking practice (zor se bolo)

1. This laptop is mine, and that one is yours.
2. Whose phone is this? — It's hers.
3. My house is bigger than my neighbour's house.
4. This exam was more difficult than the last one.
5. She is the luckiest person I know.
6. He is better at coding than I am.
7. This is the most interesting book I have ever read.
8. The weather today is worse than yesterday.
