# 🗣️ English Speaking — Zero to Confident

> Banaya gaya: ek backend developer ke liye jo technically strong hai, par **English bolne** mein confidence chahiye — interviews, presentations aur daily work ke liye.

Yeh full track hai: **Basic → Intermediate → Advanced**. Har topic Hindi/Hinglish explanations ke saath hai taaki samajhna asaan ho, lekin lakshya (goal) hamesha **English mein bolna** hai.

---

## 📌 Sabse pehle padho ye 3 rules

1. **Roz bolo, zor se (out loud).** Sirf padhna kaafi nahi. Har lesson ke "🎤 Practice" section ko awaaz mein bolo.
2. **Recording karo.** Phone pe roz 2-min ka ek voice note. Khud sun-na = sabse fast feedback.
3. **Perfect mat socho, fluent socho.** Galti karo, par rukko mat. Fluency > grammar accuracy (shuru mein).

## 📅 Roz ka 30-minute routine

| Time | Kaam |
|------|------|
| 10 min | Aaj ka lesson padho + samjho |
| 10 min | Lesson ki sentences **zor se bolo** (🎤 Practice section) |
| 5 min | Phone pe 2-min voice note record karo, phir sun-na |
| 5 min | 5 naye shabd / 1 idiom / 1 phrasal verb yaad karo |

Hafte mein 1 baar: Claude ke saath ek **mock conversation / mock interview** (English mein likh ke ya bol ke). Main feedback dunga.
