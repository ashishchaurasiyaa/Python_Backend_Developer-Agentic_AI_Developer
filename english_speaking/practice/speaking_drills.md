## 1️⃣ Shadowing — sabse powerful drill

**Kya hai:** Native speaker ka audio/video chala ke, unke peeche-peeche **same time pe** bolo — jaise unki parchhai (shadow) ho.

**Improves:** Accent, intonation, rhythm, natural speed. Yeh single best drill hai accent ke liye.

**Time:** 10 min · **Frequency:** Daily

**Step-by-step:**
1. Ek short clip chuno (30–60 sec). Source: TED talks, YouTube tech talks, podcast clips, ya English news. Tech-related lo to double fayda.
2. **Pehle sirf suno** — ek baar pura, samajhne ke liye.
3. **Phir subtitles ON karke** clip chalao aur saath-saath bolo — speaker ko match karne ki koshish karo (pause na karke, live).
4. Pakad na aaye? 0.75x speed pe chalao.
5. Same clip **5 baar** repeat karo. Har baar smooth hoga.
6. Aakhri baar **subtitles OFF**, sirf audio + apni awaaz.

> Tip: Tone aur "music" copy karo, sirf words nahi. English ki apni ek lay (rhythm) hoti hai — woh pakdo.

## 2️⃣ Recording Yourself — 2-minute daily voice notes

**Kya hai:** Roz ek prompt pe 2 minute bolo, phone pe record karo, wapas suno.

**Improves:** Fluency, self-awareness (apni galti khud sunoge), confidence.

**Time:** 5 min · **Frequency:** Daily

**Step-by-step:**
1. Niche list se aaj ka prompt chuno (ya order me chalो).
2. Phone ka voice recorder kholo. **Bina script** 2 min bolo.
3. Atak gaye? Ruko mat — "umm" bolo, sambhal ke aage badho. Flow > perfection.
4. Recording **wapas suno**. Ek cheez note karo jo improve karni hai (ek pronunciation ya ek grammar).
5. Recording delete mat karo — Week 1 aur Week 4 ki compare karna.

## 3️⃣ Mirror Practice

**Kya hai:** Sheeshe ke saamne khade ho ke bolo — apni aankhon me dekhte hue.

**Improves:** Confidence, eye contact, facial expressions, body language (interview ke liye gold).

**Time:** 5 min · **Frequency:** 3x/week

**Step-by-step:**
1. Sheeshe ke saamne khade ho jao. Posture seedhi.
2. Koi topic chuno (list #6 se) aur **khud se baat** karo, jaise saamne koi insaan ho.
3. Apni aankhon me dekho — neeche/idhar-udhar nahi.
4. Smile karo jab natural lage. Haath se gestures use karo.
5. Awkward lagega — yahi point hai. Camera/interview se pehle yahan comfortable bano.

## 4️⃣ Narrate-Your-Life / Think-Aloud

**Kya hai:** Jo bhi kaam kar rahe ho, usko English me **live commentary** ki tarah bolo.

**Improves:** Spontaneous speaking, "thinking in English", har-roz ki vocabulary.

**Time:** 5 min (ya kabhi bhi) · **Frequency:** Daily

**Kaise:**
- Chai banate hue: *"I'm boiling the water. Now I'm adding tea leaves. I should add sugar…"*
- Code likhte hue: *"I'm writing a function that takes two arguments. First, I'll validate the input…"*
- Bina ruke bolo — ghar pe akele, koi nahi sun raha. Galti ki tension zero.

> Yeh drill brain ki "translate Hindi→English" habit todti hai aur seedhe English me sochना sikhati hai.

## 5️⃣ Read-Aloud

**Kya hai:** Koi bhi English text zor se padho — clear aur slow.

**Improves:** Pronunciation, sentence flow, breathing, confidence.

**Time:** 10 min · **Frequency:** Daily

**Step-by-step:**
1. Source chuno: tech blog, documentation, news article, ya aaj ki lesson.
2. **Slow padho.** Speed nahi, clarity goal hai.
3. Punctuation pe ruko — comma pe chhota pause, full-stop pe bada.
4. Mushkil word aaye? Ruko, 3 baar bolo, phir aage.
5. Bonus: ek paragraph **record** karke wapas suno (Drill #2 ke saath combine).

> Tech docs read-aloud karna double-win hai: technical vocab + pronunciation, saath-saath.

## 6️⃣ The "1-Minute on a Topic" Drill

**Kya hai:** Random topic uthao, 60 second **continuously** bolo — bina rukе, bina script.

**Improves:** Fluency under pressure, quick thinking in English, interview ke "tell me about X" jaise sawaalon ke liye.

**Time:** 5 min (2–3 topics) · **Frequency:** Daily

**Step-by-step:**
1. Niche se ek topic chuno (aankh band karke point karo).
2. Timer 60 sec set karo. **Start.**
3. Lagataar bolo. Atke? "Let me think…", "Another point is…" jaise fillers use karke flow banaye rakho.
4. Goal: poora minute bina lambe silence ke.
5. Roz 2–3 topic. Daily + tech mix karo.

## 7️⃣ Describe-an-Image Drill

**Kya hai:** Koi bhi photo uthao aur usko detail me describe karo — jaise andhe insaan ko samjha rahe ho.

**Improves:** Vocabulary, descriptive language, detail dekhne ki aadat.

**Time:** 5 min · **Frequency:** 3x/week

**Step-by-step:**
1. Image chuno: phone gallery ka koi photo, ya Google pe "random scene".
2. **Top-down** describe karo: *"This is a picture of… In the foreground there is… In the background…"*
3. Colours, positions, emotions, kya ho raha hai — sab bolo.
4. Naya word chahiye? Ruko nahi — simple word se kaam chalao, baad me sahi word dhoondo.
5. Useful phrases: *"It looks like…", "In the centre…", "On the left…", "It seems that…"*

## 8️⃣ Self-Talk in English

**Kya hai:** Apne mann ki awaaz (internal monologue) ko English me switch karo.

**Improves:** "Thinking in English", instant fluency, translate-karne ki aadat khatam.

**Time:** All day, free · **Frequency:** Always

**Kaise:**
- Plan banate waqt mann me: *"Okay, first I'll finish this task, then I'll review the PR."*
- Kuch reaction ho: *"That's interesting", "I'm not sure about this", "Let me check."*
- Subway/walk pe akele ho? Halki awaaz me bolo.

> Yeh "free" drill hai — koi extra time nahi lagta, par sabse zyada compound effect deta hai. Din me jitni baar yaad aaye, switch karo.

## 9️⃣ Mock Interview with Claude

**Kya hai:** Claude ke saath real interview simulate karo — woh sawaal puchega, aap bolke (ya likh ke) jawab do, aur woh feedback dega.

**Improves:** Interview readiness, tech-in-English, pressure handling, fluency.

**Time:** 20 min · **Frequency:** 2x/week

**Setup — Claude ko yeh prompt do:**

```
You are a senior backend interviewer. Conduct a 20-minute mock interview
with me. I'm an Indian backend developer practising SPOKEN English.

Rules:
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer.
- Mix behavioural ("tell me about a time…") and technical
  (system design, APIs, databases) questions.
- After each answer, give SHORT feedback: 1 grammar fix,
  1 better phrase, and a fluency tip.
- Keep me talking — push for "why" and "how".
- At the end, give an overall rating and my top 3 things to improve.
```

**Kaise use karo:**
1. Upar wala prompt paste karo.
2. Sawaal aaye — pehle **bolke** answer banao (out loud), phir type/dictate karo.
3. Feedback padho, ek improvement note karo.
4. Session ke end ki "top 3" list ko agle hafte target banao.

> Pro tip: Phone ke voice-to-text se answer dictate karo. Isse aap actually **bologe**, sirf type nahi karoge — aur drill ka asli fayda yahi hai.
