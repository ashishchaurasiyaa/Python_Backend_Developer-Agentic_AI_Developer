## 🎤 Kaise use karein

1. Ek sub-topic roz chuno (12 sentences).
2. Har sentence 2 baar zor se bolo — pehli baar dekh ke, dusri baar bina dekhe.
3. Apne asli kaam/project ke context mein badal ke bolo.
4. Tag questions wale do sub-topics ek saath practice karo — positive→negative tag aur negative→positive tag dono side se comfortable ho jao.
