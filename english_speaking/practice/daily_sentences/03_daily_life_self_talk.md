## Narrating the day out loud (self-talk drill)

| # | Sentence to SAY | Matlab (Hinglish) | Tone | Note / trap |
|---|------------------|--------------------|------|--------------|
| 1 | So far today I've had breakfast, cleared my inbox and joined standup. | Aaj ab tak naashta kiya, inbox saaf kiya aur standup attend kiya. | casual | Present perfect drill: 'today so far' ke liye 'I've + V3'. |
| 2 | Right now I'm sitting at my desk, waiting for the build to finish. | Abhi main desk par baitha hoon, build khatam hone ka wait kar raha hoon. | casual | Present continuous for what is happening this moment. |
| 3 | Yesterday I worked late, so this morning I started an hour later. | Kal der tak kaam kiya, isliye aaj subah ek ghanta late shuru kiya. | casual | Past simple drill: finished time = 'yesterday'. |
| 4 | By the time I got home, everyone had already eaten. | Jab tak main ghar pahuncha, sab log khaana kha chuke the. | casual | Past perfect: pehle wali action ke liye 'had + V3'. |
| 5 | I've been living in this flat for about three years now. | Is flat mein karib teen saal se reh raha hoon. | casual | 'For three years', not 'since three years'. |
| 6 | Tomorrow I'm meeting a friend for lunch, it's already fixed. | Kal ek dost se lunch par mil raha hoon, plan pakka hai. | casual | Fixed future plan = present continuous, not 'will meet'. |
| 7 | If it rains this evening, I'll skip the gym and stretch at home. | Agar shaam ko baarish hui, to gym chhod ke ghar par hi stretch karunga. | casual | First conditional: 'if + present', 'will + verb'. |
| 8 | I used to cook every night, but these days I mostly order in. | Pehle roz raat ko khaana banata tha, ab zyadatar order kar deta hoon. | casual | 'Used to' = pehle karta tha, ab nahi. |
| 9 | I should have gone to bed earlier. I'm paying for it today. | Mujhe jaldi so jaana chahiye tha. Aaj bhugat raha hoon. | casual | 'Should have + V3' = pachtava, jo nahi kiya. |
| 10 | I'm going to clean the kitchen after this call, I promise. | Is call ke baad kitchen saaf karunga, pakka. | casual | 'Going to' = pehle se socha hua irada. |
| 11 | The clothes are still drying, so I'll fold them in the evening. | Kapde abhi sookh rahe hain, shaam ko tah kar dunga. | casual | - |
| 12 | Nothing much happened today, and honestly that's fine. | Aaj khaas kuch nahi hua, aur sach mein koi baat nahi. | casual | Good closing line for a daily spoken journal. |
