## STAR - structuring your story

## Closing the interview

| # | Sentence to SAY | Matlab (Hinglish) | Tone | Note / trap |
|---|------------------|--------------------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Thanks, this was really useful. I've got a much clearer picture now. | Shukriya, yeh baat-cheet sach mein kaam ki rahi. Ab mujhe kaafi saaf tasveer mil gayi hai. | neutral | Simple, warm closing - over-thanking mat karo. |
| 2 | I'm definitely interested. What are the next steps from here? | Mujhe is role mein pakka interest hai. Yahan se aage ke steps kya hain? | neutral | Say this explicitly. Many candidates forget to show interest. |
| 3 | Is there anything about my background you'd like me to clarify before we wrap up? | Khatam karne se pehle mere background ke baare mein kuch clear karna ho to bataiye? | formal | Strong closing question - lets you fix any doubt on the spot. |
