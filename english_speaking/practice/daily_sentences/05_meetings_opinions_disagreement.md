|---|------------------|--------------------|------|--------------|
| 1 | I think we've covered everything. Anything else before we wrap up? | Lagta hai sab cover ho gaya. Khatam karne se pehle aur kuch? | neutral | 'Wrap up' = finish. Standard closing phrase. |
| 2 | Let's leave it there. Thanks, everyone. | Yahin khatam karte hain. Sabka shukriya. | neutral | - |
| 3 | We're at time, so let's stop here and continue on Slack. | Time ho gaya, to yahin rokte hain aur Slack pe aage baat karenge. | neutral | - |
| 4 | Thanks for your time. I'll follow up in writing. | Aapke time ka shukriya. Likhit mein bhej dunga. | formal | Good closer for client calls. |
| 5 | Let's pick this up again on Thursday, same time. | Thursday ko usi time pe isko phir se uthaate hain. | neutral | - |
| 6 | That's all from me. Have a good one. | Mere taraf se itna hi. Din achha jaaye. | casual | 'Have a good one' works any time of day. |
| 7 | I'll get the notes out within the hour. | Ek ghante ke andar notes bhej dunga. | neutral | - |
| 8 | If anything's unclear later, just ping me. | Baad mein kuch samajh na aaye to seedha message kar dena. | casual | - |
| 9 | Great discussion. I think we finally have a plan. | Achhi baat-cheet rahi. Lagta hai aakhirkar plan ban gaya. | neutral | - |
| 10 | We're done early for once, so I'll give you ten minutes back. | Aaj jaldi khatam ho gaya, to das minute aapke hi rahe. | casual | Ending early makes people genuinely happy. Say it out loud. |

## Client calls - pushing back and setting expectations

| # | Sentence to SAY | Matlab (Hinglish) | Tone | Note / trap |
|---|------------------|--------------------|------|--------------|
| 1 | I want to be upfront about the timeline. This is four weeks, not two. | Timeline ke baare mein saaf-saaf keh doon. Ye chaar hafte ka kaam hai, do ka nahi. | formal | 'Upfront' = honest from the start. |
| 2 | We can absolutely do that, but something else will have to slip. | Ye ho jaayega, par phir koi doosri cheez peeche khisak jaayegi. | neutral | Never just say no. Say yes with a trade-off. |
| 3 | Let me check with the team before I commit to a date. | Koi date dene se pehle team se puch loon. | formal | - |
| 4 | That's a bigger change than it sounds. Can I come back with an estimate? | Ye sunne mein chhota lagta hai par bada change hai. Estimate bhej doon? | formal | - |
| 5 | Just so expectations are clear, this release doesn't include reporting. | Ummeedein saaf rakhne ke liye bata doon, is release mein reporting nahi hai. | formal | - |
| 6 | I'd rather promise Friday and deliver than promise Tuesday and miss. | Tuesday bol ke miss karne se accha hai Friday bolun aur de doon. | neutral | - |
| 7 | Can we split it? Core flow now, the rest in the next release. | Isko baant dein? Main flow abhi, baaki agle release mein. | neutral | - |
| 8 | I understand it's urgent. Let me tell you what's realistic. | Samajh raha hoon ki urgent hai. Main bata deta hoon kya mumkin hai. | formal | Acknowledge the pressure first, then give the real answer. |
| 9 | We hit an issue on our side, and I wanted to flag it early. | Hamari taraf ek dikkat aayi hai, isliye pehle hi bata raha hoon. | formal | Bad news early is respected. Bad news late is not. |
| 10 | Thanks for flagging that. I'll look into it first thing tomorrow. | Batane ke liye shukriya. Kal subah sabse pehle dekhta hoon. | formal | Say this, not 'kindly do the needful' - that reads as dated. |
