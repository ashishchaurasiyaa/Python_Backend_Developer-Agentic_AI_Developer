# 📅 Daily Routine — 30-Day English Speaking Plan

A 30-minute-a-day plan jisse 30 din me aap apni spoken English ko clearly upar le ja sakte ho — bina apni tech study chhode.

---

## 🧠 Philosophy — pehle yeh padho (2 min)

Spoken English ek skill hai, knowledge nahi. Aap grammar "jaante" ho — problem yeh hai ki mooh se nahi nikalti. Skill sirf **bolne se** aati hai, padhne se nahi.

Teen rules jo sab kuch decide karte hain:

| Rule | Hinglish me matlab | Kyun |
|------|--------------------|------|
| **Consistency > Intensity** | Roz 30 min > Sunday ko 5 ghante | Brain ko repetition chahiye, dumping nahi. 1 din 5 ghanta karke 6 din gayab = zero progress. |
| **Speak OUT LOUD daily** | Mann me mat bolo — awaaz nikaalo | Tongue aur jaw ko muscle-memory banani hai. Silent reading se mooh train nahi hota. |
| **Record karo, suno** | Apni awaaz record karke wapas suno | Galti tabhi pakdoge jab khud ko third-person ki tarah sunoge. Yeh thoda awkward hai — yahi growth hai. |
| **Done > Perfect** | Aaj galat bolo, par bolo | Perfect bolne ka wait kiya to kabhi nahi bologe. Fluency pehle, polish baad me. |

> Ek line yaad rakho: **"Main roz bolunga, chahe kitna bhi tूta-phoota ho."** Yahi pura plan hai.

## ⏱️ Daily 30-Minute Breakdown

Roz ka same structure — taaki sochna na pade, bas karna pade.

| Time | Block | Kya karna hai |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **0–5 min** | 🔥 Warm-up | Aaj ki lesson se 5 sentences zor se padho. Mooh kholo, slow bolo. |
| **5–15 min** | 📖 Learn | Din ki assigned lesson padho + 3 naye phrases/words note karo. |
| **15–25 min** | 🎤 Speak drill | Ek drill karo (`speaking_drills.md` se) — shadowing ya 1-minute topic. |
| **25–28 min** | 📱 Record | 2-minute voice note record karo aaj ke topic pe. |
| **28–30 min** | ✅ Review | Apni recording sun lo, ek galti note karo, tracker me tick karo. |

> Sirf 30 min hai. Timer laga lo. Block khatam = ruk jao. Over-doing se burnout aata hai.

## 🚑 "Aaj miss ho gaya" — kya karein

Miss hona normal hai. Quit karna problem hai. Yeh rules follow karo:

- **2-minute rule:** Bilkul time nahi? Sirf 1 voice note record karo (2 min). Streak zinda. Done > perfect.
- **Never miss twice:** Ek din miss = thik hai. **Do din lagातार miss = danger.** Agle din chahe 5 min hi karo, par karo.
- **Miss ki bharpai mat karo:** Kal ka double aaj mat karo. Burnout aata hai. Bas aaj wala karo, aage badho.
- **Reset, don't restart:** Tracker me "X" mat socho — bas next box tick karne pe focus karo. Pichla bhool jao.

---

## 💪 "Chhodne ka mann kar raha hai" — padho yeh

Har seekhne wale ko Day 10–15 ke aas-paas lagta hai "kuch nahi ho raha." **Yeh normal hai — yahi woh point hai jahan log quit karte hain aur isliye majority kabhi fluent nahi hoti.** Aap minority me raho.

| Jab feel ho… | Yaad rakho |
|--------------|------------|
| "Progress nahi dikh raha" | Day 1 ki recording sun. Difference saaf hai. Progress slow par real hai. |
| "Awkward lagta hai khud ko sunna" | Yeh awkwardness hi growth ki nishani hai. Comfortable log nahi seekhte. |
| "Time nahi hai" | 30 min nahi to 5 min. Kuch > kuch nahi. |
| "Galtiyan bahut karta hoon" | Galti = data. Galti karega tabhi sudharega. Native bachche bhi 1000 galtiyan karte hain. |
| "Akela bore lagta hai" | Mock interview Claude ke saath karo (`speaking_drills.md` me setup). |

> 30 din baad aap wahi insaan honge jo aaj bolne se darta tha — bas ab confidence ke saath bolega. **Bas roz bolte raho.**
