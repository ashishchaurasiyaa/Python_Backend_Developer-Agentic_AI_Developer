# 💬 Daily Communication Practice — Lesson 5–8 Combined

> Lesson 5–8 (Awal Sir) ke saare points — commas, not, and, is/are + contractions, personal pronouns, demonstrative pronouns, WH questions — akele-akele nahi, **ek saath roz ki baat-cheet mein** use hote hain. Yeh file un sabko real dialogues/scenarios mein jodti hai, taaki practice "daily communication" jaisa lage, exercise jaisa nahi.
>
> Source lessons: [lesson_05_06](../Awal_Course_Notes/lesson_05_06_describing_state_is_are.md) · [lesson_07_08](../Awal_Course_Notes/lesson_07_08_pronouns_wh_questions.md)

## 🧩 Kaunsa point kahan use hota hai

| Situation | Use hone wale points |
|---|---|
| Apna introduction dena | is/am, commas (list), and, personal pronouns |
| Kisi cheez/room ko describe karna | this/that/these/those, it, is/are |
| Kisi se sawaal poochna | WH questions (What/Where/When/Who/How/Why) |
| Apna mood/status batana | is/are + not, contractions (isn't, aren't, I'm not) |
| Kisi doosre insaan ke baare mein batana | he/she/they, is/are, commas |

---

## 1️⃣ Scenario: Naye colleague se milna (Self-introduction)

**Combines:** is/am, commas, and, personal pronouns

> **You:** Hi, I'm Ashish. I'm a backend developer, and I work on the payments team.
> **Colleague:** Nice to meet you! What do you do exactly?
> **You:** I build APIs, fix bugs, and review code. It's busy, but I like it.
> **Colleague:** That sounds interesting. Where do you sit?
> **You:** I sit near the window. That's my desk over there.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apna introduction 3 sentences mein do — ek "I'm ___", ek comma-list, ek "and" wala. Zor se bolo.

---

## 2️⃣ Scenario: Apna room/desk describe karna

**Combines:** this/that/these/those, it, personal pronouns, is/are

> This is my desk. It's a bit messy today. This is my laptop, and that is my water bottle. These are my notes, and those are old files I need to clean up. It's a small space, but it's mine.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne kamre/desk ko 5 sentences mein describe karo — this, that, these, those, aur it — sab use karo.

---

## 3️⃣ Scenario: Kisi se pehli baar baat karna (Small talk with WH questions)

**Combines:** WH questions, personal pronouns, is/are

> **You:** Hi! What's your name?
> **New person:** I'm Priya. What about you?
> **You:** I'm Ashish. Where do you work?
> **New person:** I work at a startup downtown. How about you?
> **You:** I work remotely. When did you join this team?
> **New person:** Last month. Why do you ask?
> **You:** Just curious — you seem new here, so I wanted to say hi.

🗣️ **Try it**: Kisi (imaginary) naye insaan se 6 WH questions poochho — ek-ek What, Where, When, Who, How, Why. Zor se bolo, jaise woh saamne khada ho.

---

## 4️⃣ Scenario: Apna mood/status batana (negation + contractions)

**Combines:** is/are + not, contractions, and, commas

> **Manager:** How are you today?
> **You:** I'm tired, but I'm not stressed. I'm not behind on work, and I'm not worried about the deadline.
> **Manager:** Good to hear. Is the team ready for the demo?
> **You:** They're ready. They aren't fully done, but they're close.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apna aaj ka mood 3 sentences mein batao — kam se kam ek "not"/contraction use karke (isn't, aren't, I'm not).

---

## 5️⃣ Scenario: Ek teammate ko describe karna (third person)

**Combines:** he/she/they, is/are, commas, and

> My teammate Riya is smart, hardworking, and friendly. She is not always available, but she helps when she can. Our team lead, Vikram, is calm and confident. He and Riya work well together. They are a good pair.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne kisi teammate/friend ko 3 sentences mein describe karo — comma-list ke saath, aur "he" ya "she" use karke.

---

## 6️⃣ Full mini-dialogue — sab kuch ek saath (office standup)

**Combines:** har point ek chhoti conversation mein

> **Manager:** Good morning! How is everyone today?
> **You:** I'm good, but I'm a bit tired. This is my third meeting today.
> **Manager:** What did you work on yesterday?
> **You:** I fixed a bug, wrote tests, and reviewed a PR. It's done now.
> **Manager:** Great. Where are we on the payments feature?
> **You:** It's almost ready. It isn't fully tested yet, but it's close.
> **Manager:** Who is helping you with testing?
> **You:** Riya and Vikram are helping. They're not finished, but they're close too.
> **Manager:** Why is it taking longer than planned?
> **You:** The API changed, and it wasn't documented well. That's the main reason.

🗣️ **Try it**: Yeh dialogue zor se, dono roles bolke practice karo (khud manager, khud employee). Phir apna khud ka version banao apne asli kaam ke baare mein.

---

## ✍️ Mixed Practice — fill in the blanks

1. This is my laptop, and _____ is my phone. (that)
2. She is busy, _____ she is not stressed. (but)
3. _____ do you live? — I live in Pune.
4. They _____ (are/is) coming to the party tonight.
5. He _____ (isn't/aren't) feeling well today.
6. _____ is your favourite hobby? — Reading books.
7. These are my books, and _____ are my notes. (those)
8. I'm tired, _____ I'm not giving up. (but)

**Answers:** 1. that · 2. but · 3. Where · 4. are · 5. isn't · 6. What · 7. those · 8. but

---

## ✅ Quick recap — daily use cheat-sheet

| Kaam | Kya use karo |
|---|---|
| Apna intro | I'm ___, comma-list, and |
| Cheez describe karo (paas) | this / these + is/are |
| Cheez describe karo (door) | that / those + is/are |
| Sawaal poochho | What/Where/When/Who/How/Why |
| Mood/status (negative) | isn't / aren't / I'm not |
| Kisi third person ke baare mein | he/she/they + is/are + comma-list |

## 🎤 Speaking practice (roz zor se bolo)

1. I'm a backend developer, and I work on the payments team.
2. This is my desk, and that is my teammate's desk.
3. What is your name, and where do you work?
4. I'm not stressed, but I'm a bit tired today.
5. She is smart, hardworking, and friendly.
6. They aren't finished yet, but they're close.
7. Who is helping you, and why is it taking longer?
8. This is my third meeting today, and it isn't over yet.

🗣️ **Daily challenge:** Roz subah, apne aaj ke plan ko 5 sentences mein bolo — kam se kam ek comma-list, ek "not"/contraction, ek this/that, aur ek WH question (khud se ya kisi doosre se poochne wala) use karke.
