## 2026-09-03 — Impetuous

**Part of Speech:** Adjective
**English Meaning:** Acting quickly without thinking.
**Hindi Meaning:** जल्दबाज़ / उतावला।
**Synonyms:** rash, impulsive
**Antonyms:** cautious, careful

**Sentence:**
- His impetuous decision caused problems.
- उसके जल्दबाज़ फैसले ने समस्या पैदा कर दी।
