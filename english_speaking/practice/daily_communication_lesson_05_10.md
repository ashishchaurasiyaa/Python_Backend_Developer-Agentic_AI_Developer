## 1️⃣ Scenario: Naye colleague se milna (Self-introduction)

**Combines:** is/am, commas, and, personal pronouns (Lesson 5, 7)

> **You:** Hi, I'm Ashish. I'm a backend developer, and I work on the payments team.
> **Colleague:** Nice to meet you! What do you do exactly?
> **You:** I build APIs, fix bugs, and review code. It's busy, but I like it.
> **Colleague:** That sounds interesting. Where do you sit?
> **You:** I sit near the window. That's my desk over there.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apna introduction 3 sentences mein do — ek "I'm ___", ek comma-list, ek "and" wala.

---

## 2️⃣ Scenario: Apna room/desk describe karna (with 'the')

**Combines:** this/that/these/those, it, is/are, use of 'the' (Lesson 7, 9)

> This is my desk, and **the** chair next to it is broken. It's a bit messy today. This is my laptop, and that is my water bottle. These are my notes, and those are old files I need to clean up. **The** window behind me lets in good light — it's a small space, but it's mine.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne kamre/desk ko 5 sentences mein describe karo — this/that/these/those, it, aur kam se kam ek "the" use karo (jaise "the window", "the door").

---

## 3️⃣ Scenario: Kisi se pehli baar baat karna (yes/no + information + WH questions)

**Combines:** yes/no questions, information questions, WH questions, personal pronouns (Lesson 7, 9)

> **You:** Hi! Are you new here?
> **New person:** Yes, I joined last week.
> **You:** Nice! What's your name?
> **New person:** I'm Priya. What about you?
> **You:** I'm Ashish. Where do you work, and how many people are on your team?
> **New person:** I work in design, and there are five of us. Do you like working here?
> **You:** Yes, I do. Why do you ask?
> **New person:** Just curious — you seem to know everyone already.

🗣️ **Try it**: Kisi (imaginary) naye insaan se pehle 2 yes/no questions poochho ("Are you...?", "Do you...?"), phir 2 WH questions ("What...?", "Where...?"). Zor se bolo.

---

## 4️⃣ Scenario: Kisi baat ko confirm karna (Tag Questions) — naya

**Combines:** tag questions (Lesson 9)

> **Manager:** You finished the report, don't you?
> **You:** Yes, I did — it's ready for review.
> **Manager:** Great. And Riya is helping with testing, isn't she?
> **You:** Yes, she is. She's not fully done, but she's close.
> **Manager:** The demo is tomorrow, isn't it?
> **You:** Yes, it is. We can't postpone it, can we?
> **Manager:** No, we can't. Let's stay on track.

🗣️ **Try it**: 3 statements banao apne kaam ke baare mein, phir har ek mein sahi tag jodo — "..., isn't it?" / "..., don't you?" / "..., can't he?". Yaad rakho: positive statement → negative tag, negative statement → positive tag.

---

## 5️⃣ Scenario: Apne office/city ke baare mein baat karna ('the')

**Combines:** use of 'the' (Lesson 9)

> The office I work in is in **the** city center. **The** cafeteria on **the** third floor has good food. Yesterday, we visited **the** park near **the** office for a team lunch. **The** manager who runs our team is very supportive.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apni city/office ke baare mein 4 sentences bolo, har ek mein "the" use karke (jaise "the mall", "the station", "the meeting room").

---

## 6️⃣ Scenario: Apna mood/status batana (negation + contractions + negative sentences) — updated

**Combines:** is/are + not, contractions, negative sentences (not/never/none/neither...nor) (Lesson 5, 9)

> **Manager:** How are you today?
> **You:** I'm tired, but I'm not stressed. I don't have any blockers, and I never leave things for the last minute.
> **Manager:** Good to hear. Is the team ready for the demo?
> **You:** They're ready. Neither of us found any bugs, and none of the tests are failing.
> **Manager:** Perfect — that's not something I hear every day!

🗣️ **Try it**: Apna aaj ka mood/status 4 sentences mein batao — kam se kam ek contraction (isn't/aren't/don't/doesn't), aur ek "never"/"none"/"neither...nor" use karke.

---

## 7️⃣ Scenario: Ek teammate ko describe karna (third person)

**Combines:** he/she/they, is/are, commas, and, negative sentence (Lesson 7, 9)

> My teammate Riya is smart, hardworking, and friendly. She is not always available, but she helps when she can. She doesn't like being interrupted during focus time. Our team lead, Vikram, is calm and confident. He and Riya work well together — they are a good pair, and they never miss a deadline.

🗣️ **Try it**: Apne kisi teammate/friend ko 4 sentences mein describe karo — comma-list, "he"/"she", aur ek negative sentence (doesn't/never) ke saath.

---

## 8️⃣ Full mini-dialogue — sab kuch ek saath (office standup, Lesson 5–10)

**Combines:** har lesson ek chhoti conversation mein

> **Manager:** Good morning! How is everyone today?
> **You:** I'm good, but I'm a bit tired. This is my third meeting today.
> **Manager:** What did you work on yesterday?
> **You:** I fixed a bug, wrote tests, and reviewed a PR. It's done now.
> **Manager:** Great. Where are we on the payments feature?
> **You:** It's almost ready. It isn't fully tested yet, but it's close. The remaining part is just documentation.
> **Manager:** Who is helping you with testing?
> **You:** Riya and Vikram are helping. They're not finished, but they're close too. Neither of them found any major bugs.
> **Manager:** You're confident about the release date, aren't you?
> **You:** Yes, I am. We won't miss the deadline.
> **Manager:** Why did it take longer than planned?
> **You:** The API changed, and it wasn't documented well. That's the main reason — it's not something we could have avoided.
> **Manager:** Fair enough. The team is doing well, isn't it?
> **You:** Yes, it is. None of us are worried about the deadline anymore.

🗣️ **Try it**: Yeh dialogue zor se, dono roles bolke practice karo. Phir apna khud ka version banao — kam se kam ek tag question aur ek negative sentence apne asli kaam ke baare mein daalke.

## ✍️ Mixed Practice — fill in the blanks (Lesson 5–10)

1. This is my laptop, and _____ is my phone. (that)
2. She is busy, _____ she is not stressed. (but)
3. _____ do you live? — I live in Pune. (Where)
4. They _____ (are/is) coming to the party tonight. (are)
5. He _____ (isn't/aren't) feeling well today. (isn't)
6. You like this project, _____? (don't you)
7. She is your manager, _____? (isn't she)
8. _____ chair by the window is mine. (The)
9. I have _____ seen that movie. (never)
10. _____ of them can attend the meeting. (None)
11. He speaks _____ Hindi _____ English fluently — wait, he speaks both! (Rewrite: He speaks neither French nor Spanish.)
12. We're not behind schedule, _____? (are we)

**Answers:** 1. that · 2. but · 3. Where · 4. are · 5. isn't · 6. don't you · 7. isn't she · 8. The · 9. never · 10. None · 11. Neither...nor · 12. are we

## ✅ Quick recap — daily use cheat-sheet (Lesson 5–10)

| Kaam | Kya use karo | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Apna intro | I'm ___, comma-list, and | 5, 7 |
| Cheez describe karo (paas/door) | this/these vs that/those + is/are | 7 |
| Ek specific cheez point out karo | the | 9 |
| Haan/na wala sawaal poochho | Is/Do/Are...? | 9 |
| Specific info wala sawaal poochho | What/Where/When/Who/How/Why | 7, 9 |
| Kisi baat ko confirm karo | statement, tag? (don't you/isn't she/can't he) | 9 |
| Mood/status (negative) | isn't / aren't / don't / doesn't | 5, 9 |
| Kabhi nahi / koi nahi batao | never / none / neither...nor | 9 |
| Kisi third person ke baare mein | he/she/they + is/are + comma-list | 7 |

## 🎤 Speaking practice (roz zor se bolo)

1. I'm a backend developer, and I work on the payments team.
2. This is my desk, and the chair next to it is broken.
3. Are you free tomorrow, and what time works for you?
4. You finished the report, don't you? — Yes, it's ready.
5. The meeting room on the second floor is booked for us.
6. She isn't stressed, but she's a bit tired today.
7. None of us are worried, and neither is the manager.
8. He plays the guitar, doesn't he? — Yes, he does, every weekend.
9. They aren't finished yet, but they're close.
10. The team is doing well, isn't it? — Yes, it is.

🗣️ **Daily challenge:** Roz subah, apne aaj ke plan ko 6 sentences mein bolo — ek comma-list, ek "the", ek tag question, aur ek negative sentence (not/never/none/neither) zaroor use karo.
