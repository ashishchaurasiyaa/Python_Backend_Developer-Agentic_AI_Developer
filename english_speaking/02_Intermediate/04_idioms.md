# 🗣️ Idioms — common English idioms jo log roz bolte hain

Idioms wo phrases hain jinka literal matlab kuch aur hota hai, par real matlab alag. Inhe samajhna zaroori hai kyunki native speakers conversations, meetings aur calls mein inhe **roz** use karte hain. Yahan sirf wahi idioms hain jo aaj bhi genuinely bole jaate hain — koi purane/Shakespeare-wale nahi.

> 💡 **Padhne ka tareeka:** Pehle Hindi meaning dekho, fir example loud bolo. Idiom ko ek poore sentence mein bolne ki aadat daalo — tabhi wo zubaan par chadhega.

## ⚠️ Interview mein idioms — carefully use karo

Idioms aapko fluent dikhate hain, par interview mein **thoda sambhal ke**:

> ✅ **Correct (natural):** "We were on a tight deadline, so we had to **bite the bullet** and prioritise the core features."
> ❌ **Avoid (forced/overused):** "It was a piece of cake, I nailed it, I was over the moon, it was a blessing in disguise..." — ek hi line mein 4 idioms thoonsna fake lagta hai.

Rules:
- **1-2 idioms per answer** is plenty. Naturally aaye to bolo, force mat karo.
- **Safe in interviews:** *on the same page, get up to speed, learning curve, edge case, ballpark figure, raise the bar, root of the problem.* Ye professional hain.
- **Avoid in formal answers:** *hit the sack, freak out, down in the dumps* — ye casual hain, friends ke saath theek hain.
- Idiom ka matlab 100% confirm hone par hi bolo. Galat idiom bolne se confusion hota hai.

> ❌ **Common mistake:** "I am very exciting for this role." (galat — aap exciting nahi ho, situation exciting hai)
> ✅ **Correct:** "I am very **excited** about this role." / "This role sounds **exciting**."

## 🎤 Practice (zor se bolo)

Har sentence ko 2 baar loud bolo. Pehle padh ke, fir bina dekhe yaad se.

1. "This bug fix was a **piece of cake** — it took me five minutes."
2. "Before we start, let's make sure we're all **on the same page**."
3. "Can you bring the new developer **up to speed** on our codebase?"
4. "We forgot to handle the **edge case** where the input is empty."
5. "I was **over the moon** when I cleared the system design round."
6. "Don't **beat around the bush** — just tell me the root cause."
7. "It's **crunch time** before the release, so we're all focused."
8. "The new framework has a steep **learning curve**, but I'm getting the hang of it."
9. "Let's **weigh the pros and cons** before we pick the database."
10. "We fixed it **in the nick of time**, right before the demo."
11. "Thanks for the **heads-up** about the maintenance window."
12. "That outage was a **blessing in disguise** — it exposed our weak monitoring."

**🎯 Mini speaking task:**
Apne kisi **real project ka 60-second story** bolo, jisme kam se kam **4 idioms** naturally aayein. Suggested: start with *"It was crunch time..."*, beech mein *"we had to think outside the box"* aur *"we were back to square one"*, aur end *"...but we pulled it off in the nick of time."* Record karo aur sun-na — kya idioms natural lage ya thoonse hue?
